# Battlefield sound pack

Drop your sound files here, then reopen the Battlefield game window. WAV and MP3 are recommended; OGG works when supported by the browser. Files must be under 8 MB; effects play for at most 3 seconds.

Supported filenames (choose .wav, .mp3 or .ogg):
- cast: spell or ability added to the stack
- priority: your priority begins
- spawn: permanent enters the battlefield
- attack / block / damage / death
- buff / debuff / leave
- stack-exit: stack item leaves (resolution and countering share this event)

Example: attack.wav, damage.mp3, priority.wav. WAV takes precedence if multiple formats exist. Missing or unsupported sounds use synthesized defaults where available. Sounds play only while Battlefield is focused, with a short cooldown to keep crowded turns quiet. Adjust Effects sound volume in Settings; 0 mutes Battlefield.

XMage's regular sounds mute while a Battlefield window keeps reporting its presence, including when it is in the background. They restore automatically after the window closes (within about 2.5 seconds). Your XMage sound preferences are unchanged.

Installed pack: original procedural fantasy effects, generated by Mage.Client/renderer/scripts/make-sound-pack.py. No third-party samples are used.
